Steps

  1. Connect the phone to the same wifi as this computer.
  2. Scan the QR, or open Chrome and type the address above.
  3. Sign in with that person's own staff account.
  4. Bookmark it / "Add to Home screen" so nobody types it again.
Keep in mind
  • This computer must stay on and awake — closing the app stops it for everyone.
  • The address changes if the router hands this computer a new one. If phones suddenly cannot connect, open this page again and check the number.
  • On the wifi the app runs over plain http, so phones cannot "install" it as an app or cache pages — they do not need to, the server is right here. Anything typed while a phone briefly loses wifi is still queued and syncs by itself.
  • Only devices on your wifi can reach it. It is not on the internet.
