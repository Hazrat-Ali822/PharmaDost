🚀 Getting started (everyone)

{{ branding.brand_name|default:"Sehatyar" }} is a complete hospital & pharmacy system. What you see in the left menu depends on your role — you only get the screens you need.

Sign in

  1. Open the website (or the installed app) and enter your email and password.
  2. You land on your own dashboard — a summary made for your role.

Find your way around

  • Left sidebar — every screen you can open, grouped (Clinical, Pharmacy, Finance, System, App). Click a group heading to expand or collapse it.
  • Top bar — the hospital name/logo, notifications (🔔) and your account menu.
  • Theme — switch light/dark with the toggle; your choice is remembered on this device.
  • Notifications — a red dot means something needs you (a prescription to fill, a patient to admit, a bill to collect). Click it to see the list.

Install it as an app

  1. Go to App → Get the App in the sidebar.
  2. Follow the prompt to add it to your phone's home screen or desktop.
  3. It then opens like a normal app, with the hospital's own name and logo.
Tip: the whole system works on a phone — reception, the ward round and the pharmacy counter are all designed for a small screen.
🧾 Front desk / Reception

Used by: Receptionist, Admin

Reception is where every visit begins. The system asks one question: is this a new patient or an old one?

A brand-new patient

  1. Open Reception → New Visit (or Patients → Register).
  2. Pick the department first — only that department's doctors are then offered.
  3. Fill the patient details. You can type either the age or the date of birth — the other fills in automatically. CNIC dashes are added as you type.
  4. Choose the doctor (only doctors currently sitting are shown; tick the box to see everyone for an emergency).
  5. Save. In one step this registers the patient, books the appointment and raises the consultation fee.
  6. The printable token slip opens — hand it to the patient.

A returning patient

  1. Open Reception → New Visit and search by MRN, mobile, CNIC or name. Typing the CNIC straight off the card works.
  2. Select the patient, choose department + doctor, and book. The token slip prints as before.
The patient number (MRN) is given automatically — you never type it. Each hospital has its own numbering.
🩺 Doctor / OPD

Used by: Doctor

See your patients

  1. Your dashboard shows today's queue — patients booked to you, in token order.
  2. Open a patient to see their history: past visits, prescriptions, lab & imaging results, admissions.

Write a prescription

  1. On the visit, choose Write Prescription.
  2. Add medicines (with dose, duration, instructions). Allergy and duplicate warnings appear automatically.
  3. Use a saved prescription template to add a common set of medicines in one click.
  4. Save — it goes straight to the pharmacy's pending queue.

Order tests & scans

  • Add lab tests or imaging from the visit; each order automatically raises a bill and notifies the lab / radiology.

Advise admission or surgery

  • Use Advise Admission or Advise Surgery. This sends a request to reception / the OT queue — they confirm the bed or schedule the operation. The patient stays yours.

Set your availability

  • From the OPD board, one click marks you on leave / available for a given day. This is per-date, so today's leave never hides you tomorrow.
💊 Pharmacy

Used by: Pharmacist, Wholesale, Admin

Sell at the counter (POS)

  1. Open Pharmacy → New Sale.
  2. Search and add medicines; set quantity. The price fills in automatically.
  3. Add any discount; choose the payment method and the amount paid (blank = full).
  4. Save — stock is reduced automatically (earliest-expiry batch first) and the receipt is ready to print.

Fill a doctor's prescription

  1. The pending prescriptions list shows what doctors have prescribed.
  2. Click one — the sale screen opens with the medicines pre-loaded.
  3. Sell all of them and the prescription is marked dispensed; sell some and it stays as partial.

Stock & batches

  • Medicines lists your catalogue with live stock. Expired batches are never sellable.
  • The system warns you about low stock and near-expiry items.

Purchase orders (buying stock)

  1. Open Purchase Orders → New. Add items (paste a list, quick-add, or repeat a past order); low-stock items can be suggested for you.
  2. Send the order to the supplier, then Receive it to add the stock in.

Wholesale / bulk orders

  • Wholesale lets you build a large order sheet fast and convert it to a bill in one click. Wholesale pricing needs a registered customer.
Returns go back to their batch; anything that came back expired is kept aside and cannot be re-sold.
🧪 Laboratory

Used by: Lab Technician, Admin

  1. Open Lab → Orders to see tests doctors (or the ward) have ordered — each already has a pending bill.
  2. Open an order and enter the results; save.
  3. Print the report on the hospital letterhead; the doctor sees the result on the patient's history.

Prices

  • Admin sets test prices under Price List → Lab Tests. These prices are used automatically when a test is ordered.
🩻 Imaging / Radiology

Used by: Sonographer / Radiologist, Admin

  1. Open Imaging → Studies to see scans that have been referred (each already billed).
  2. Open a study and write the report; save and print on the letterhead.

Prices

  • Admin sets scan prices under Price List → Scans / Imaging.
🛏️ Inpatient & Ward (IPD)

Used by: Doctor, Nurse (Ward Staff), Ward In-charge, Admin

Admit a patient

  1. When a doctor advises admission, reception sees it in the queue and confirms — pick the ward/bed and attending doctor to create the admission.
  2. Wards & Beds is where you set up rooms, beds and daily rates.

Doctor rounds

  • Doctors record a round (assessment/plan) on the admitted patient. Doctors see this as My Inpatients.

Nursing care (Ward Staff)

  • Record Vitals — temperature, pulse, BP, breathing, oxygen, consciousness, pain, blood sugar. The system scores an early-warning number (MEWS) and colours it green / amber / red so a deteriorating patient stands out.
  • Fluid I/O — record intake and output; the balance is totalled for you.
  • Nursing Note — your shift progress note. Care Task — log routine care (turning, hygiene, catheter/IV/wound care, mobilising, feeding).
  • Give Medication — record a dose. If it's a pharmacy medicine it reduces stock and is added to the discharge bill; the patient's own medicine and off-catalogue drugs are recorded without a charge.

Shift change & the whole ward

  • Handover — write an end-of-shift SBAR handover; the incoming nurse reads the Handover Board and acknowledges taking over.
  • Nursing Board — every inpatient with their latest early-warning score, sickest first — the "who needs attention now" screen.
  • Ward Census — admissions, discharges and occupancy for a day.

Running the ward (Ward In-charge)

  • Duty Roster — build the weekly shift roster (morning/evening/night) for each nurse.
  • Patient Allocation — assign each admitted patient to a nurse who is rostered for that shift; each nurse's load is shown.
  • A nurse sees their own shifts and today's patients under My Duties.

Discharge

  1. Open the admission and choose Discharge.
  2. The bill is prepared automatically: bed charges for every day plus every medicine given.
  3. The printable discharge summary (stay, diagnosis, rounds, medicines, itemised bill) opens.
⚕️ Operation Theatre (OT)

Used by: Doctor (advises), OT staff, Admin

  1. A doctor uses Advise Surgery; it lands in the OT queue.
  2. OT staff confirm it to create the surgery record and schedule it.
  3. Record the procedure details; the charge is invoiced automatically.
  4. Procedures holds the standard operations and their charges.
🚑 Emergency / Casualty
Used by: Reception, Doctors, Nurses, Admin

The casualty desk — register a walk-in or ambulance case in seconds, triage it, and track what happens to it.

Register a case

  1. Open Emergency / Casualty and start a new case.
  2. Pick an existing patient, or type a name to quick-register a new one (finish the paperwork later).
  3. Set the triage colour — Red (immediate), Yellow (urgent), Green (non-urgent). The board sorts sickest-first.
  4. Tick MLC for any police / accident (medico-legal) case and add the MLC number.

Work the board

  • Open a case to set its disposition — in-treatment, admitted, discharged, referred, LAMA or expired.
  • A consultation fee can be charged at intake.
Triage first, paperwork after — the ER screen is built for speed.
🤰 Maternity / ANC
Used by: Doctors, Nurses, Admin

Antenatal care, deliveries and the birth register.

Antenatal (ANC)

  1. Open Maternity → Register pregnancy and enter the LMP — the expected date of delivery (EDD) and weeks are worked out for you.
  2. Add each checkup under the pregnancy — BP, weight, fundal height, fetal heart rate.

Delivery & birth register

  • Record the delivery (type, outcome, who conducted it). Add one row per baby — twins are two rows.
  • The birth register lists every delivery; a delivery charge can be raised.
🩺 Diagnoses (ICD-10)
Used by: Doctors, Admin

Attach a standard ICD-10 code to a patient so diagnoses are structured and report-ready, not just free text.

  • Open Diagnoses, pick the patient and search the code by name or number.
  • Admin can extend the code list under the ICD catalogue; common codes are pre-loaded.
↪️ Referrals (in / out)
Used by: Doctors, Reception, Admin

Send a patient to another hospital, or record one referred in.

  1. Open Referrals → New referral, choose the patient and the other facility.
  2. Write the reason and a short clinical summary (investigations, treatment given).
  3. Open the referral and press Referral Letter to print it on the hospital letterhead.
🩸 Blood Bank
Used by: Lab, Doctors, Nurses, Admin

Donors, blood-unit stock and issue to patients.

Stock

  • Register donors, then add units (bag number, group, component, expiry). Tick screening done.
  • The dashboard shows available units by blood group and what is expiring within a week.

Issue

  • Issue a unit to a patient — the cross-match is noted and the bag is marked issued.
  • An out-of-date bag is never offered, even if it was never flagged.
💉 Vaccination / EPI
Used by: Doctors, Nurses, Admin

Record immunizations against the national EPI schedule.

  • Open Vaccination, pick the child and the vaccine, record the dose, batch and next-due date.
  • Print the child's immunization card from their record.
  • The Due / Overdue list shows who needs to come back.
📜 Birth & Death Certificates
Used by: Reception, Doctors, Admin

Issue and reprint birth and death certificates.

  • Fill the form (a patient record isn't required — a newborn usually has none) and save; each gets its own serial number.
  • Print it on the hospital letterhead; reprint any time from the register.
✍️ Consent Forms
Used by: Doctors, Nurses, Admin

Record a signed patient consent and print it with signature lines.

  1. Admin keeps reusable templates (surgery, anaesthesia, blood transfusion…).
  2. Open Consent → New consent, pick the patient and a template — the wording is copied onto the record so a later template edit never changes what was signed.
  3. Print it; it carries signature lines for the patient/guardian, a witness and the doctor.
💵 Billing & Finance

Used by: Receptionist, Accountant, Admin

Collect a patient's payment

  1. Open Billing → Patient Billing and search the patient (it defaults to everyone who owes money).
  2. Open their bill — it shows every charge (consultation, lab, imaging, pharmacy, admission) in one place.
  3. Enter the amount and method, and Receive Payment (it clears the oldest items first).

Print / send the bill

  • Print Bill puts it on the hospital letterhead — with a scannable QR of the summary if turned on.
  • Send on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with the bill summary ready to send to the patient — no setup needed.

Invoices, expenses & the day book

  • Service invoices carry a proper number (e.g. INV-2026-00001). An admin can void an invoice (this is recorded).
  • Expenses records money going out (rent, salaries, utilities…).
  • Cash Closing / Day Book reconciles the drawer at day-end: expected vs counted cash.
  • Doctor Payouts records fees paid to doctors.
🪪 Panels / Insurance / Sehat Card
Used by: Admin, Accountant, Reception

Bill institutional payers — private insurance, corporate panels and the govt Sehat Card.

Set up & link

  1. Add each payer under Panels (name, type, contact, co-pay %).
  2. Tick which services this card covers — OPD, Pharmacy, Lab, Imaging, IPD, Procedures. Leave all boxes unticked to cover everything. So one card can be "OPD only", another the inpatient package (IPD + lab + imaging but not the pharmacy counter), another the whole hospital.
  3. At registration, link a patient to their panel and card / policy number. A per-patient coverage limit (e.g. a Sehat Card annual limit, in rupees) can also be set — the panel pays up to that amount, the rest is the patient's.

Claims & payments

  • Every bill for a covered patient is checked against the card's covered services: a covered service becomes a claim the panel owes, automatically; anything the card does not cover is billed to the patient as normal. Nothing extra to tick at the counter.
  • Record panel payments; the ledger shows every claim, payment and the running balance, and allocates each payment across the oldest open claims.
📊 Reports

Used by: Admin, Accountant

  • Dashboard shows today's income by department (OPD, lab, imaging, pharmacy) and the attention list.
  • Analytics / Visual Analytics show sales trends, best/dead-stock movers and profit (from the cost captured at each sale).
  • Reports can be filtered by day / week / month.
👥 Users & Access (Admin)

Used by: Admin

Add a staff member

  1. Open System → Users → New.
  2. Enter their details and pick a role (Doctor, Nurse, Pharmacist, Receptionist, Lab Tech, Sonographer, Accountant, Wholesale, Admin). The role decides what they can see.
  3. Save and give them their password.

Fine-tune what someone can do

  • In Edit user → Access you can switch individual features on or off for that one person, beyond their role's defaults.
  • Example: promote a senior nurse to Ward In-charge by granting the roster/allocation feature.
👥 Staff HR (attendance, leave, payroll)
Used by: Admin, Accountant

The people side — profiles, attendance, leave and salaries.

  • Keep a profile per staff member (designation, monthly salary, joining date).
  • Mark attendance from a daily grid — present / absent / leave / half day; re-saving a day updates it.
  • Handle leave requests (approve / reject), and pay salaries with a printable payslip.
⚙️ Settings & Branding (Admin)

Used by: Admin

Open System → Settings. Everything here is per-hospital.

Brand

  • Set the name, tagline, logo and colours. Tick "pick the theme colour from the logo" to match your app to your logo automatically. A live preview updates as you type.
  • Choose the default theme (light / dark / follow the device) and the print design for bills & reports.

Money

  • Currency symbol — shown before every amount (Rs, PKR, $…).
  • Tax, discount & rounding — an optional tax % on every bill, a standing discount % on pharmacy sales, and rounding the total to the nearest 1/5/10. All start at zero, so nothing changes until you set them.
  • Invoice numbering — your prefix (e.g. INV) and whether the year appears in the number.
  • Patient numbering (MRN) — your prefix and the running number.

Modules

  • Turn whole modules on/off (pharmacy, OPD, IPD, OT, lab, imaging, finance, reports). An off module disappears from everyone's menu.

Backup

  • Download Backup saves your whole database and uploaded files as one .zip. Keep it somewhere safe.
📶 Install the app & work offline

Used by: everyone

1. Install it on your phone (do this once)

Installing puts {{ branding.brand_name|default:"Sehatyar" }} on your home screen like a normal app — it opens instantly and keeps working when the internet drops. It is the same login, just quicker.

  • Android (Chrome): open the site → tap the ⋮ menu (top-right) → Add to Home screen → Install. An icon appears on your home screen.
  • iPhone (Safari): open the site → tap Share (the □↑ box) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  • Computer (Chrome/Edge): an Install icon appears at the right of the address bar — click it.
  • Not sure? Open App → Get the App in the menu — it checks your device and shows a button to save every screen for offline.
Open the app once while you do have internet, and tap through the screens you use (reception, POS, etc.). That saves them so they still open when the internet is gone.

2. When the internet drops

Just keep working — nothing stops.

  • A form you save offline is queued on your device, not lost, and sent to the server automatically the moment the internet is back.
  • The Offline Queue (App → Offline Queue) shows what is waiting to sync, and anything that was rejected — with the reason, and a Try again / Discard button.
  • A visit registered offline prints a provisional slip the patient carries to the doctor. The real MRN and token are issued once it syncs.
  • Numbers (MRN, token, bill numbers) are always given by the server at sync — never guessed on the device, so two phones can never clash.

Good to know

  • Each phone keeps its own queue. While the internet is down, what reception enters on their phone reaches the doctor's phone after it syncs, not instantly.
  • You can only open screens you visited at least once while online — that is why step 1's "tap through your screens" matters.
For a clinic with no internet at all, the desktop build can act as a LAN server: every phone on the same wifi runs the whole system live — including instant notifications between phones — with no internet. Ask your admin.
